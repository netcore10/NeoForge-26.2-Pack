AUTOMOBILITY: AUTORADIO — CUSTOM STATION

Add .ogg, .mp3 or .wav files up to 64 MiB to this folder. The station
appears in the radio wheel after at least one supported file is present.

Recommended filename: Artist - Title.ext
Rename the station safely in station.json or in the in-game manager.
Files directly in this folder form the original default playlist.
Each direct subfolder is another playlist, named after its folder.
Deeper folders and symbolic links are ignored. Select a playlist in
the in-game manager; Custom remains one entry in the radio wheel.
At most 16 playlists and 250 playable tracks total are discovered.
